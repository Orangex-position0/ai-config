# Cover Type and Template Selection

Use this mapping in order after the Content Brief and before selecting a Style Recipe. First select one existing Cover Type; then select its cover template and matching people reference. A user-selected Cover Type or template overrides the recommendation.

| Video type | Content center | Cover Type | Cover Template | Recommended recipe | People reference | Title guidance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Concept explanation or architecture principle | One mechanism or idea | `Concept Poster` | `Concept Poster` | `Minimal Concept` or `Dark Technical Split` | `posture-3`, `posture-6`, or none | 2–5 concrete words |
| Debugging or diagnosis | Failure point and signal | `Signal Focus` | `Signal Focus` | `Dark Technical Split` | `posture-4` or `posture-7` | Name the pain or signal |
| Refactoring, migration, or performance improvement | Before/after shift | `Problem Versus Solution` | `Problem Versus Solution` | `Dark Technical Split` | `posture-4` or none | Show the change, not the full process |
| Tool tutorial, setup, or AI agent demo | Workflow and result | `Workbench` | `Workbench` | `Dark Technical Split` or `Human-Centered Tech` | `posture-5`, `posture-9`, or `posture-6` | Name the tool or outcome |
| Opinion, experience, or project retrospective | Personal point of view | `Character Plus Concept` | `Character Plus Concept` | `Human-Centered Tech` | `posture-7`, `posture-8`, or `posture-2` | Use a direct claim or tension |
| Comparison or review | Choice and trade-off | `Problem Versus Solution` | `Problem Versus Solution` | `Editorial Poster` or `Human-Centered Tech` | `posture-2` or `posture-4` | Name both sides only if still legible |
| Method, checklist, or engineering practice | High-signal takeaway | `Concept Poster` or `Signal Focus` | `Concept Poster` or `Signal Focus` | `Minimal Concept` | `posture-6` or none | Prefer a short imperative |

## Selection procedure

1. Identify the video type from the table and select exactly one Cover Type.
2. Select the mapped cover template from `references/templates.md`.
3. Select at most one matching people reference, or explicitly choose no person.
4. Adapt the pair to the platform and available safe area.
5. Record the Cover Type, template, person choice, and Style Recipe in the composition proposal before generation.

Do not use the table to force a person into a technical-first cover. The content metaphor and thumbnail readability take priority over variety.
