# Platforms

Use the same brand anchors across platforms, but change composition and density for the viewing context.

## Long-Form 16:9

Use for YouTube, Bilibili, course videos, and default video thumbnails.

- Make the hook readable at small sizes.
- Use one dominant subject and one short title block.
- Keep logo, ID, or series name secondary.
- Leave enough negative space for compression and mobile browsing.

## Bilibili

Bilibili covers can carry more personality than a strict professional blog cover.

- Allow stronger hook wording, expressive illustration, creator identity, and playful visual rhythm.
- Keep technical meaning visible; do not let character art become the entire message.
- Use high contrast and clear hierarchy because the cover competes in dense feeds.

## YouTube

YouTube thumbnails need immediate contrast and a strong focal path.

- Make one emotional or conceptual promise visible in under a second.
- Prefer 2-4 words on the cover when possible.
- Use faces only when personality is part of the channel brand or video value.

## Shorts, Reels, TikTok

Vertical short-video covers need directness.

- Use a large subject, large text, and very few words.
- Let a person, object, or single metaphor become the visual entry point.
- Avoid small diagrams, small code, and multi-part explanations.

## Technical Blogs, Zhihu, Juejin

Blog-adjacent covers should feel clearer and more professional.

- Emphasize the technical concept, not spectacle.
- Use minimal illustration, concept graphics, simple UI fragments, or restrained typography.
- Avoid exaggerated emotion and platform-trend decoration when it weakens credibility.

## Cross-Platform Adaptation

For each target platform, keep:

- Core brand colors or contrast logic.
- Typography family or title treatment.
- Logo, ID, border, frame, or recurring symbol.
- The same core content metaphor.

Change:

- Aspect ratio and crop.
- Text length and hierarchy.
- Subject scale.
- Emotional intensity.
- Amount of technical detail.
