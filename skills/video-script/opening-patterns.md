# Opening Patterns For 技术讲解类

Choose one main pattern for the opening. The opening should quickly answer: what is the problem, why is it worth attention, and what will the viewer gain?

## 痛点型

Use when the target viewer is likely experiencing a specific problem.

Structure:

```text
你是否遇到过……
↓
这个问题会导致……
↓
本期视频将帮助你……
```

Good for tool tutorials, coding pitfalls, performance optimization, engineering practice, and workflow changes.

## 结果预览型

Use when the final effect or before/after contrast is visually strong.

Structure:

```text
这是最终效果
↓
这是改造前后的差异
↓
接下来解释如何实现
```

Good for refactoring, UI or website development, optimization results, automation tools, configuration changes, and from-scratch builds.

## 反常识型

Use when the video corrects an incomplete common belief.

Structure:

```text
很多人认为 A
↓
但真正的问题不是 A，而是 B
↓
本期视频解释其中原因
```

Good for principles, architecture, misconception correction, best-practice analysis, and solution comparison.

## 代码问题型

Use when a short code sample can reveal a hidden risk or misunderstanding.

Structure:

```text
展示一段代码
↓
代码看起来没有问题
↓
提出隐藏风险
↓
本期视频分析原因和解决方案
```

Good for language features, bug analysis, code review, async or concurrency, performance issues, and API misuse.

## 场景故事型

Use when the concept is easier to understand through a concrete system or project scenario.

Structure:

```text
假设你正在做……
↓
开始时系统很简单
↓
随着需求增加，问题逐渐出现
↓
本期视频分析如何解决
```

Good for system architecture, engineering incidents, project evolution, technical selection, distributed systems, and longer technical explainers.

## 提问型

Use when the topic can be framed as a direct question with a non-obvious answer.

Structure:

```text
提出一个核心问题
↓
给出常见答案或直觉判断
↓
指出问题比想象中更复杂
```

Good for concept explainers, technical selection, principles, comparison videos, and interview-style knowledge.

## 对比冲突型

Use when two or more plausible options solve different problems.

Structure:

```text
方案 A vs 方案 B
↓
两者都能解决问题
↓
但适用条件完全不同
↓
本期视频给出选择依据
```

Good for frameworks, architecture choices, language feature comparisons, performance strategies, and migrations.

## 结论先行型

Use when the video's claim is clear and information density matters.

Structure:

```text
直接给出结论
↓
说明结论解决了什么问题
↓
接下来提供证据和推导过程
```

Good for technical opinions, best-practice summaries, engineering lessons, tool recommendations, short videos, and dense explainers.
