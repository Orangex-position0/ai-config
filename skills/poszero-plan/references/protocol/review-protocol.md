# Review Protocol

Review the contract, not every generated line.

## Review Packet fields

- changed decisions and requirements;
- required human review items;
- risk and likely consequence;
- unresolved questions;
- automated checks and evidence;
- deferred content that does not need line-by-line review.

## Review scope

- For `change.md`, review a material scope or risk change before implementation.
- For `spec.md`, obtain direct approval of the Spec, Plan, and Tasks revisions at their gates. Review irreversible or externally visible decisions when they arise in Specify or Plan.
- For Validation, present FAIL, BLOCKED, manual evidence, and risk acceptance to the user.

Persist human decisions, risk acceptance, and exceptions in the owning document. Do not create a standalone Review Packet file.
Discussion-stage closure is separate from document approval.
